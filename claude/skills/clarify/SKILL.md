The user does not follow what you just said.

**Write the entire output in Japanese**, even though this file is written in English.

## Steps

1. Summarise in one sentence what you have been explaining up to now.
2. State, in one sentence, your own reading of what the user actually wants to know.
3. Check whether those two match.
4. Where they may not match, confirm it rather than filling in the gap yourself.
5. Rebuild the explanation in this order:
   - the conclusion
   - why it follows
   - a concrete example
   - what to do in this particular case
6. Keep fact, inference and proposal clearly apart.
7. Cut unnecessary jargon, abstract phrasing and side discussions.
8. Where an earlier explanation was wrong or imprecise, correct it explicitly.

## Watch for

- Never let "generally speaking" carry a conclusion on its own.
- Never answer at length about something adjacent instead of the question asked.
- Never invent a plausible premise to fill a missing one.
- When you say "A, therefore B", spell out how A leads to B.
- When your own explanation has grown complicated, reset its structure and explain again rather
  than adding more.
